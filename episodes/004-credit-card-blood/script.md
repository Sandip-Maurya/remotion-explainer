# Episode 004: The Credit Card in Your Blood

## Narrative Director Cues & Audio Board

### Beat 1: The Human Hook (0–7s)
* **Voiceover:** "Every seven days, without tasting it or seeing it, you eat an entire plastic credit card."
* **Visual:** Shot 1 — Macro spotlight on dinner plate with crumbling credit card dissolving into glowing granules next to silver fork.
* **SFX:** `sub-drop` (f0) -> `cash-register` (f30)
* **Callout:** "5 GRAMS EVERY WEEK" (Warning Amber)

### Beat 2: The Agitation & Sensitization (7–22s)
* **Voiceover:** "Sounds impossible, right? We've all seen the heartbreaking photos: sea turtles tangled in plastic ghost nets, and endless mountains of trash choking our coastlines. We thought all that plastic stayed out in the ocean. It didn't."
* **Visual:** Shot 2 — Sea turtle entangled in abandoned nylon ghost nets under murky sea. Transition to Shot 3 — Mountain of plastic kachra / trash choking a coastline.
* **SFX:** `whoosh` (f220) -> `pop` (f420)
* **Callout:** "THE RETURNED WASTE" (Coral Red)

### Beat 3: The Hidden Mechanism (22–50s)
* **Voiceover:** "Ocean waves and ultraviolet sunlight slowly crush that waste into microscopic fragments. First microplastics, then nanoplastics—particles so unimaginably small they slip straight through your gut wall and enter your bloodstream. Scientists now find synthetic polymers inside human lungs, hearts, and even brain tissue. That is five grams of plastic every single week. Two hundred and fifty grams a year. By age seventy, you've absorbed over twenty kilograms of industrial polymers."
* **Visual:** Shot 4 — Fluorescent cellular microscopy of glowing synthetic fibers inside red blood cells. SVG lifetime accumulation exponential curve (0 -> 20 kg).
* **SFX:** `whoosh` -> `pop` -> `clock-tick` -> `pop`
* **Callout:** "20 KG OVER A LIFETIME" (Electric Sky Blue)

### Beat 4: The Climax & Dramatic Pause (50–62s)
* **Voiceover:** "And here is the terrifying realization: we didn't just turn the oceans into a landfill. We turned ourselves into one."
* **Visual:** Pitch black void, singular shimmering micro-polymer particle floating under a harsh cold spotlight.
* **Audio:** Complete music cut (45 frames dead silence) on realization -> heavy `sub-drop` on "into one".
* **Callout:** "WE ARE THE LANDFILL" (Pure White / Flash)

### Beat 5: The Payoff & Seamless Loop (62–72s)
* **Voiceover:** "You can't clean the planet overnight. But avoiding single-use plastics and filtering your drinking water cuts your intake dramatically. Protect your body. Because every single week..."
* **Visual:** Glass filtration metaphor icon + action card + EndCard outro.
* **SFX:** `chime` -> smooth loop bridge to Beat 1.
* **Callout:** "PROTECT YOUR WATER" (Vibrant Emerald)
