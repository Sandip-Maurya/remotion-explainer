Thirty-five trillion dollars... That is thirty-five million millions. Right now, every single American citizen owes over one hundred thousand dollars of national debt.

Back in the year 2000, that number was just five trillion. Today, interest payments alone eat up over one trillion dollars every single year—costing more than the entire defense budget.

So who actually lent all this money? China and Japan own about eight trillion. The Federal Reserve holds five trillion. But the single biggest lender? It's you... Your pension, your bank deposits, and your social security.

The danger isn't that America can't pay. It's how they pay.

They quietly print more currency—eroding the purchasing power of every dollar you save.

When debt grows faster than real value, it's not a loan anymore... It's a stealth tax on your time.
